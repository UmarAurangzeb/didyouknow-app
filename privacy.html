Privacy Policy

DidYouKnow?? Studio · Last updated 10 November 2026

What this app is

DidYouKnow?? Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the DidYouKnow?? YouTube channel to upload videos they made to their own channel.

Google data it uses

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube read-only permission (youtube.readonly): to read the owner's own channel and video details (titles, upload and processing status, view counts) so the tool can confirm an upload succeeded and avoid repeating topics.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and all video data stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other user, viewer or commenter.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
